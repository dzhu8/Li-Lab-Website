Cell-cell communication & Biological Patterns.

We integrate synthetic biology, developmental biology, biophysics and systems biology to reveal how cell-cell communication gives rise to spatial and temporal biological patterns that underlie the fundamental organization of life.

Highlighted Publications

Diffusion barriers imposed by tissue topology shape Hedgehog morphogen gradients

Using single-molecule imaging, we determined that Sonic hedgehog transitioned between membrane-confined and unconfined states aided by SCUBE1, identifying a mechanism by which morphogen diffusion is controlled.

Reconstructing signaling histories of single cells via perturbation screens and transfer learning

We developed an integrated experimental-computational framework that learns morphogen signaling response signatures from a high-throughput in vitro perturbation atlas and infers combinatorial signaling activities in in vivo cell types.
